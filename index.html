How to use

Adding and updating patients

Tap + to add a patient. Sliders start at normal values, so drag to the number; the ✕ beside a slider clears it back to blank ("not recorded"). Pressing Save also files today's numbers as a snapshot, which is what feeds the trend graphs.

Reading the list

Each patient's headline shows bed, initials, age and sex, operation, consultant or ward badge, and days since surgery. Tap the headline to open the essentials (drains and jobs), tap Data for obs, bloods, CXR and medications, and tap Notes for free text that saves as you type. Tap the headline again to collapse.

Drains

Each drain has a location (pleural with apical, basal or other; mediastinal with optional anterior or posterior) and a type. Topaz drains record air leak as a number in ml/min; Rocket drains use descriptors like swing only or continuous bubbling. Any active leak shows red, and a clamped drain shows a red CLAMPED flag.

Wards and views

Swipe left or right anywhere to slide between wards, or use the bar at the bottom. In Settings (the gear, top right) you can view the list by location or by consultant; in consultant view each page gathers one consultant's patients from every ward, with the ward abbreviated in the header. Wards and consultants are both customisable from the same menu.

Trends

Once a patient has been saved on two or more days, the Trends button on their card shows graphs of drain readings, bloods and obs over time, plus a table of previous days.

Generate list

Settings, then Generate list, builds an Excel workbook with one sheet per ward in a handover layout and opens the share sheet so you can save or send it.

Your data

Everything is stored only on this phone and nothing is uploaded anywhere. Use initials and bed numbers only, never full names or hospital numbers. Treat any exported file as patient data. Discharging a patient permanently deletes them and their history, and clearing Safari website data will erase the whole list, so generate a list regularly as a backup.
